Single purpose: remove study friction on app.gizmo.ai by unlocking unlimited hearts and hints on standard quizzes and removing Magic Import's client-side cooldown.

This extension intercepts Gizmo's JavaScript bundle as it loads, applies targeted patch rules locally to remove those three client-side limits, and injects the result so users can study without interruption. Practice-exam hints are not supported, and the extension does not modify Gizmo's servers or account state.

The extension operates exclusively on app.gizmo.ai. It has no settings UI, no background data collection, and no effect on any other site or page.
